BONUS ROUND · LOREwhy orphans are always !is_current

Could an orphan slot ever hold current replicas? No. Replicas carry a composite FK to deployment_regions(deployment_id, region) — a v5 replica can only exist in a region v5 declared. A slot missing from desired can therefore only contain superseded-deployment replicas, and they land in OutgoingReplicas: exactly the drain lane.
